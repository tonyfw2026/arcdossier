## Arc Network (ARC (proposed / not publicly launched))

Circle-supported, EVM-compatible stablecoin-native L1 focused on financial settlement and agentic commerce, launched on September 16, 2026. USDC pays transaction fees. ARC was minted at genesis but has not been publicly launched.

### Identity and network

Arc Network (arc.io) is driven by the Circle ecosystem, built and operated through Arc Network Services LLC; EVM-compatible open L1, Arc Mainnet Chain ID 5042, public mainnet launched on 2026-09-16. Official materials must not be confused with the same-named ARC/CRCL/USDC tokens at the application layer. Current validator set is permissioned PoA.

### Product and business model

Core is a US dollar stablecoin-driven financial settlement network: native USDC as Gas, deterministic sub-second Finality, CCTP/Gateway cross-chain interoperability, StableFX, tokenized assets, payments and AI Agent. Circle integrates Arc with Agent Stack, Arc Portal, Arc Studio, and App Kits. Network fees are paid in USDC; network fees, Circle business revenue, and potential future ARC token value capture must be separated.

### Product data and growth

Official launch statement on 2026-09-16 claims over 100 applications and 100+ institutional/ecological builders on day one—official disclosure rather than individual on-chain verification. Independent block explorers have interfaces for total transactions, 24h transactions, accounts, contracts, USDC transfers and fee statistics, but current retrieval pages do not guarantee synchronized timing; retrieved cached values should not be disguised as real-time values. Next round requires fixed block number and UTC timestamp re-fetching, distinguishing transaction count/unique paying users/valid economic activity.

### Team and funding

Sponsor/supporter is Circle Internet Group (US-listed CRCL); official press release quotes Co-Founder and CEO Jeremy Allaire. Official listing includes BlackRock, DTCC, Mastercard, Visa, Standard Chartered and others participating in ecosystem/validator programs; further distinction needed between 'partnership established,' 'validator node,' 'actual application deployed,' and 'capital investment.' No independent Arc network fundraising valuation found; Circle market cap must not be treated as Arc protocol FDV.

### Tokenomics and value capture

On 2026-09-16, Circle announced completion of ARC genesis mint in the US with initial supply of 10,000,000,000. Official simultaneously clarified: this does not constitute a public offering commitment, ARC has not launched to the public, and is not Circle stock or profit-sharing rights. Whitepaper explores PoA→PoS (target 2027) staking/delegation, governance, USDC fee conversion to ARC rewards/burn mechanisms; implementation rules, allocation, public trading, token price and FDV must not be treated as current status. Arc transaction fees still use USDC.

### Arc ecosystem position and competition

Arc competes with Ethereum L2, Solana, Tempo, Stable and other settlement networks; on payments, cross-border settlement, RWA and agent economy relies on Circle USDC, CCTP/CPN channels and institutional client distribution. Advantages include native USDC fees, EVM tooling and institutional access; disadvantages/risks include permissioned validator centralization, USDC dependency, competing chain subsidies, regulatory scrutiny, and separation between DeFi/Meme activity and institutional real trading volume.

### Recent progress and attention

2026-09-16 public mainnet officially launched; same day ARC genesis mint 10 billion, official announced future PoS/Privacy/Network Sectors roadmap. 2026-10-12 official released exchange deposit/withdrawal integration guide, explaining single-block deterministic finality, USDC native asset and transfer event integration; Privacy Sector, Payment Sector >100K TPS, Agent Sector are all planned, not to be recorded as currently live capabilities.

### Risks

- Permissioned PoA validator centralization
- USDC dependency
- Future ARC token design is exploratory
- RWA/agent economy usage needs real transaction validation
- Not equivalent to Circle equity
