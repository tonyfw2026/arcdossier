THE SCREENING PIPELINE

Four checks per screen

Each step either produces evidence or records a gap. Nothing is inferred.

  1. 01
    Classify

    Contract address, official domain, or X handle. Documented non-Arc surfaces stop here.

  2. 02
    Probe

    The server fetches each published source and keeps the HTTP status, byte count, title, and SHA-256 of what came back.

  3. 03
    Prove on Arc

    Contract addresses are read with eth_getCode against the Arc RPC. Bytecode is proof; an empty result is a finding, not a guess.

  4. 04
    Score or refuse

    A score is issued only once Arc membership is proven. Otherwise the dossier shows the zeroed breakdown and the open questions.

SHIPPED IN v0.3

What is real today

Wired to live systems, not mocks.

  • Mainnet and testnet, checked separately

    Contract addresses are read with eth_getCode on Arc mainnet and Arc testnet, and the difference is stated rather than averaged away.

  • Deterministic metrics a trader can act on

    Supply, proxy and owner structure, pausability, and a bounded activity window — counts of transfers, addresses, mints and burns, all read from the chain.

  • Risk signals with their evidence attached

    Upgradeable logic, owner roles, separate admins, minting observed in the window. Each signal names the check that produced it.

  • A score you can audit line by line

    Every point maps to a check that either passed or failed, and an unproven project gets no score at all.

  1. 01

    Sort and filter by score, by proven deployment, and by risk signal — work a shortlist instead of one project at a time.

  2. 02

    Get told when a screened project's sources, contract structure or activity change, instead of re-checking by hand.

  3. 03
    Ownership, not just presence

    Require a project's X account and domain to cross-link before a social profile counts as evidence.

  4. 04
    Ranking that shows its inputs

    Ecosystem ranking built from measured screens and proven deployments, with the inputs published.

  5. 05
    Non-custodial execution

    Wallet-confirmed routes only. No keys, no custody, and no paid placement anywhere inside a dossier.

  6. 06
    Funding and team data sources

    Those two dimensions are currently marked unmeasured rather than scored. Wiring a real source is what would let them count.

EXPLICIT LIMITS

What this does not do

  • It does not tell you what to buy or sell. The score ranks how well a project's evidence holds up, not expected return. No output here is a trade recommendation, and the AI layer is structurally forbidden from producing one.
  • It does not confirm that a live website belongs to the team behind a contract. RPC proves deployment, not ownership.
  • It does not audit code, tokenomics, or intent. A deployed contract is not a good project.
  • It does not hold keys or funds, and it does not accept payment for placement.
  • Testnet deployments are labelled as testnet. A testnet contract is cheap to deploy and does not prove a project is live on Arc mainnet.
